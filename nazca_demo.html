Nazca Lawn Services: appointment confirmation and a job board

Concept demo. When a customer books a mow, cleanup, or sprinkler job, the system confirms the window by SMS. If the crew runs late, the visit is rescheduled, or the window is missed, it texts the customer automatically instead of leaving them waiting in silence. Every visit is on the day's job board, so a job cannot be "spaced." Missed calls get an automatic reply and a log entry.
