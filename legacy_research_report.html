Legacy Moving LLC: Research Report

The business and what it does

Legacy Moving LLC is a Lakewood, Colorado moving company located at 8805 W 14th Ave Ste 300, Lakewood, CO 80215-4848. Owned by Andrew McFarland, the company has been in business for 5 years, since 2021. Legacy Moving provides residential moves, commercial and office moves, storage, and loading and unloading services.

The company maintains a strong overall reputation. On Google, Legacy Moving holds a 4.8 rating across 2,000 reviews. On Yelp, the company holds a 4.3 rating across 210 reviews. The business is BBB Accredited with an A+ rating and has two complaints in the last three years, with zero complaints in the last 12 months. Customers can reach the business by phone at (720) 340-1849 or by email at info@legacymovingdenver.com. The BBB also lists an alternate phone, (970) 616-4481.

The issue

The operational problem is not the quality of the moving work itself. Rather, it is a pattern of poor communication after the move, specifically promised callbacks that never came and customers feeling dodged after filing damage claims.

The live BBB profile documents two specific complaints regarding this issue. Complaint 1, dated 07/02/2025, states: "I also called back to speak with the owner and I was told that I was gonna get a phone call back but never did." The complainant added, "after reading all the reviews online it looks like its common for this company to disappear on their clients and not respond back ever for the damages." In follow-ups on 07/15/2025 and 08/11/2025, the customer reiterated that the business never called to resolve the issue, noting, "I never got a missed call from him or his company and no voicemail. The owner completely ignored my request." In a further follow-up on 09/30/2025, the customer referred to a $0.60/lb reimbursement offer as a "scam", but this was solely one customer's description of a reimbursement rate and does not represent a BBB alert or a scam flag against the business.

Complaint 2, dated 06/25/2024, came from a repeat customer who stated their first move went without any issues. After a second move, the customer reported: "What followed was six months of frustration as [owner] dodged my calls, emails, and text messages. Desperate for a response, I pretended to be a new customer, and unsurprisingly, he was eager to take my call."

An undated Google review from Lincoln Young via moveBuddha highlights similar failures: "Communication was terrible. They did not confirm the move, arrived late, provided fewer movers than promised, damaged cabinets, and never followed up on the promised repair."

It is important to note that this problem is dated 2024 to 2025 and does not reflect a current daily failure. The business has a positive recent record. There are zero BBB complaints in the last 12 months, the latest BBB review is a 5-star rating dated 04/23/2026, and an October 2026 moveBuddha analysis shows 95% positive reviews. The issue is a real, documented historical pattern that needs a permanent structural fix, but recent reviews do not repeat it.

Why it matters

For a moving company, the move day is the highest stakes appointment a customer books all year. When things go wrong, the callback after a damage claim is the exact moment trust is kept or lost. A promised callback that never comes results in a repeat customer lost. The 06/25/2024 complainant was a satisfied repeat buyer who ultimately had to pretend to be a new customer just to get the owner on the phone. Fixing this communication gap ensures that the strong operational work in the field translates to retained customers and ongoing referrals.

The proposed solution

The proposed solution implements an automated communication protocol and a missed call tracking system.

What it does: When a customer books a move, the system sends an SMS confirming the move day and window. A day before reminder and a morning "crew en route" text follow so the customer is never left waiting in silence. If the crew runs late or the window is missed, the system texts the customer automatically. Finally, a missed call log records every missed call and every promised callback. This ensures a callback that was promised can never fall out of the process unnoticed again.

What it does not do: This solution requires no dispatch overhaul, no CRM replacement, no pricing change, and no moving work change.
