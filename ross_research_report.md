# Ross Tree Company: Research Report

## The business and what it does

Ross Tree Company is a tree care business based at 80 S Galapago St, Denver, CO 80223-1610. Owned by Mr. Gene A. Ross, the company has been in business for 48 years since its founding in 1978. It holds an A+ rating with the BBB, has been BBB Accredited since August 30, 2002, and has zero complaints in the last three years. The business holds a 4.6 star rating across 134 reviews on Google Maps. Customers can reach the business by phone at (303) 871-9121 or by email at arborist@rosstree.net.

The company provides tree trimming and removal, stump removal, plant health care, Tri-Level Tree Fertilization, emerald ash borer (EAB) treatment, and seasonal pruning. A core component of the business model is recurring repeat-visit care. Plant health care, fertilization, and EAB treatments require clients to need services again and again over time.

## The issue

The documented operational problem involves scheduling confusion and estimator no-shows where the customer only learns about the failure by calling the company to confirm the appointment. The complaints do not claim the tree work itself is bad. Instead, they highlight a communication breakdown on the front end.

The following reviews from BestProsInTown and ChamberOfCommerce document this pattern:

Linda Purcell (February 2024): "scheduling the service was poorly handled. there was confusion on the initial day, there was confusion regarding the second day, then today, we were expecting the estimator and the scheduler told us they had training that day and would not give us an estimate. I only found this out after i called to confirm the appointment. the previous no-show we also called to confirm when the estimator was 30 minutes past the scheduled arrival. maybe they can trim trees, however their scheduling system is unreliable."

F A (April 2024): "never did get an estimate from these guys despite asking them several times to send one over."

Undated review: "Scheduling felt disorganized and unprofessional. We were rescheduled twice; once with no notice and we did not learn about it until we called to ask where they were."

Undated stump-removal review: "after scheduling my appointment, they canceled on me at the last minute and told me I would have to wait potentially for weeks to get back on the schedule."

Recency note: This scheduling problem is a known past pattern to prevent, not a current daily crisis. The documented failures occurred between January and April 2024. More recent reviews show a positive current record and praise the company for its responsiveness, noting that Ross Tree Company "was very responsive and came out to look at our property twice" and "got back to us in timely manner, showed up when they said they would."

## Why it matters

For a tree care company built on recurring visits, an estimate appointment is the first step into a long-term relationship. An estimate that never happens is a customer who never gets into the system at all. A no-showed estimate is a lost repeat customer before the work even starts. Furthermore, leaving a customer waiting in silence damages trust. In the Purcell case, the customer experienced two separate scheduling failures and only learned about the no-show and the training conflict by calling the office to ask where the estimator was.

## The proposed solution

We propose an automated text-based communication flow that includes an SMS estimate-appointment confirmation, a day-before reminder, a morning "estimator en route" text, automatic no-show follow-up, and return-visit scheduling for EAB treatment and Tri-Level Fertilization clients.

What it does: The system ensures customers are never left waiting in silence. They receive proactive updates about their estimator. If a no-show does occur, the system automatically follows up to re-engage the customer instead of waiting for the customer to call the office. It also systematically keeps EAB and Tri-Level Fertilization clients on their recurring service schedules.

What it does not do: This solution requires no dispatch overhaul, no CRM replacement, no pricing change, and no change to the tree work itself.

## Scope, price, and terms

Price: $900 to $1,500 fixed fee.

Terms: 35/40/25 milestones. This breaks down to a 35% kickoff payment, a 40% payment upon acceptance of a working demo, and a 25% final delivery payment.

IP: Intellectual property transfers to Ross Tree Company on final payment.

Retainer: A $300 per month retainer upsell is available for ongoing maintenance and support.
