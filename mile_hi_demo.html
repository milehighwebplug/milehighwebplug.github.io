Mile-Hi Sprinklers: blowout and turn-on confirmation and a missed-visit log

Concept demo. When a customer books a fall blowout or a spring turn-on, the system confirms the booking by SMS, sends a day-before reminder, and texts "tech en route" on the morning of the visit. If the crew runs late, the visit is rescheduled, or the window is missed, it texts the customer automatically instead of leaving them waiting in silence. Every booked visit is on the day's job board and in the missed-visit log, so a blowout can never again be entered wrong and never scheduled.
