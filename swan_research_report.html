Swan Plumbing, Heating & Air: Research Report

The business and what it does

Swan Plumbing, Heating & Air is a family-owned LLC operating in Commerce City and Denver, CO, with additional locations in Littleton and Loveland. Owned by Andrew Swan and Timothy A. Swan, the company has been in business for 20 years, starting in March 2006.

The company provides plumbing repair, heating, and air conditioning services. A core component of the business is the Swan Club membership program. For a recurring fee, cited by one customer as $20 per month, members receive priority emergency service, annual furnace and AC tune-ups, and service discounts.

The business can be contacted at office@swanheating.com and (720) 303-2537. There is a conflict in public records regarding the primary phone number: the BBB lists (720) 399-7989 as the main line, and the Google listing shows (720) 727-6479. However, (720) 303-2537 is the dispatch number explicitly published on the live website contact page.

The issue

Overall customer sentiment for the company is highly positive. The business holds an A+ rating with the BBB, with five complaints in the last three years and none in the last 12 months. The company also holds a 4.8-star rating across 3,902 reviews on Google Maps. Customers consistently praise the field technicians and the quality of the plumbing and HVAC work.

However, there is a concentrated, ongoing failure in the membership and maintenance scheduling experience. Swan Club members frequently report no-shows, ghosting, uncommunicated schedule changes, and dropped callbacks. This is a current problem, not a historical one, as evidenced by recent BBB reviews:

Katie A, 10/29/2026 (membership customer): "On a follow-up visit, we were completely ghosted by Swan: no call, no show. When I called to ask how to reschedule, the [contact] was aggressive & rude." She also noted: "I never received a callback or acknowledgement from any of their teams after being told that it was escalated to the Install Manager."

Tim D, 12/22/2025: "They wasted my time. I setup a specific appointment time for an emergency, and they didnt even show up. No point in paying their membership fees."

Betsy K, 08/02/2024 (maintenance-program member): "They are not in time, change appointments without communicating, don't complete the work on one visit... I'm likely going to cancel my membership."

Darren E, 01/04/2024 (club member): "After routine cancellations at the last minute... I decided to cancel my membership. It's not worth the $20/month... If you are club member your routine maintenance... will never be a priority."

Marcy C, 12/29/2023 (club member 15 months): Her appointment was cancelled the morning of and rescheduled. She noted, "Appointment window was 2-5pm. Its now 7:20pm and Im still waiting."

The most recent ghosting review is dated October 29, 2026. This confirms the scheduling communication gap is an active issue directly impacting current members.

Why it matters

A membership business relies on the recurring revenue generated by its subscribers. That recurring revenue depends on tune-up visits happening on schedule and the member feeling prioritized.

When a promised follow-up visit results in a no-show or a callback request is ignored, the customer cancels their membership. Three of the five reviewers cited above explicitly cancelled or stated they were likely to cancel their subscriptions over these scheduling failures. With 3,902 Google reviews highlighting strong technical work in the field, the scheduling gap is what costs members. A forgotten tune-up equals a cancelled membership.

The proposed solution

We propose building an SMS confirmation, reminder, and missed-call recovery system paired with a simple member cancellation and no-show log.

What it does: When a Swan Club member books a tune-up or follow-up visit, the system sends an SMS confirming the appointment window. If the crew is running late, the visit is rescheduled, or the time window is missed, the system automatically texts the member so they are never left waiting in silence. The corresponding log displays the day's scheduled visits and every missed call, ensuring a callback request or a missed appointment never falls out of the operational process unnoticed.

What it does not do: This solution is strictly focused on closing the communication loop for members. It does not overhaul the dispatch system, it does not replace the current CRM, it does not change pricing, and it does not alter how the plumbing and HVAC work is performed.
