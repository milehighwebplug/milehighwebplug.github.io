Denver Ducts: appointment confirmation

Concept demo. When a customer books, the system confirms the window by SMS. If the tech runs late or the window is missed, it texts the customer automatically instead of leaving them waiting in silence. Missed calls get an automatic reply and a log entry, so nothing falls out of the process unnoticed.
