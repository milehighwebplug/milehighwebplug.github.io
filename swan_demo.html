Swan Plumbing, Heating & Air: Swan Club tune-up confirmation and a member log

Concept demo. When a Swan Club member books a tune-up or a follow-up visit, the system confirms the window by SMS. If the crew runs late, the visit is rescheduled, or the window is missed, it texts the member automatically instead of leaving them waiting in silence. Every visit is on the day's job board, and every missed call and cancelled membership lands in the member log, so a follow-up visit or a callback request can never fall out of the process unnoticed.
