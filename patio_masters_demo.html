Project status: Denver Patio Masters

A patio job runs for weeks. The customer pays a deposit and then hears nothing until the crew shows up. This demo shows status texts at each milestone and a callback log for missed calls, so no customer is left guessing and no request to speak to the owner falls out of the process.

Concept demo, not Denver Patio Masters’ system. What this fixes

  • A status text at each milestone, so the customer always knows where the job stands instead of hearing nothing for weeks after paying the deposit.
  • A missed call gets an automatic text back, so the customer is not left waiting in silence while the office catches up.
  • Every callback request is logged the moment it arrives, with a timestamp and a status, so a request to speak to the owner cannot be forgotten after a busy morning.
