Ross Tree Company: estimate confirmation and automatic no-show follow-up

Concept demo. When a customer books an estimate, the system confirms the appointment by SMS, sends a day-before reminder, and texts "estimator en route" on the morning of the visit. If the estimator no-shows, the system follows up automatically instead of waiting for the customer to call and ask where the estimator is. Every booked estimate is on the day's estimate board, every missed call and no-show is in the log, and EAB treatment and Tri-Level Fertilization clients stay on their return-visit schedule.
