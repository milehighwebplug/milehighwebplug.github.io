iPhone or iPad (Safari)

  1. Tap the Share button at the bottom of the screen.
  2. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen.
  3. Tap Add. Open it from the new icon.

Android (Chrome)

  1. Tap the three dots at the top right.
  2. Tap Add to Home screen or Install app.
  3. Open it from the new icon. Press and hold the icon to jump straight into a demo.

Computer (Chrome or Edge)

  1. Click the install icon at the right end of the address bar.
