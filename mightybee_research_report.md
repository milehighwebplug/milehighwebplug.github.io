# MightyBee Electric: Research Report

## The business and what it does

MightyBee Electric, LLC is a Denver electrician located at 7570 Lafayette St, Denver, CO 80229-6421. The business is owned by Mr. Martin L. Black, who serves as President and Master Electrician. MightyBee has been in business for 17 years, since 2009, and has held BBB Accreditation since December 2017. The company can be reached by phone at (303) 288-7988, with an alternate number of (303) 919-9722 listed on BuildZoom. The public contact email verified on the company website is mtybeeinfo@gmail.com.

The company handles panel upgrades, service upgrades, EV-charger installs, smoke-detector safety checks, surge protection, and smart-home work. The service model relies heavily on repeat customers rather than a formal subscription program. Reviewers frequently note they have used the company multiple times and plan to use them again.

The business holds a strong overall rating across platforms. On Google, MightyBee has 4.6 stars across 58 reviews. On Angi and HomeAdvisor, the company has 4.7 stars across 255 reviews. The BBB profile shows an A+ rating with zero complaints in the last three years and none in the last 12 months, and one customer review.

## The issue

The electrical work itself is not the problem, as reviewers consistently praise the actual service when it happens. The core issue is a pattern of post-booking communication failures and forgotten follow-ups. The evidence points to appointments lacking confirmation or reminders, as well as promised follow-ups being dropped, specifically the city-inspection step after a panel upgrade.

This is documented in two Angi reviews from August and December 2019 and a June 2026 BestProsInTown review:

Heather D. (Angi, December 2019): "This is a REALLY, really low-tech company. No appointment reminders, no text messages, no emails, no call ahead to let you know they're coming. I got an appointment window and that was it."

Andrew U. (Angi, August 2019): "They admitted later that they flat out forgot to follow up not only with me to confirm everything went well but with the city for the required inspection."

Unnamed Reviewer (BestProsInTown, June 2026): "Martin is the owner and not a trustworthy person. He doesn't follow up and take care of anything that he says he will do. STAY AWAY FROM THIS COMPANY. He'll just continue to duck your calls when you try to talk to him."

The exact wording from the June 2026 review also appears as the single, undated review on the company's BBB profile under the name Alex M. Note that the June 2026 reviewer's statement that the owner is "not a trustworthy person" and the directive to "STAY AWAY" is one reviewer's characterization, not a factual finding. The company maintains its A+ BBB rating with no alerts.

Recency note: This is an old pattern with a recent repeat, not a current daily failure. Recent reviews cut both ways. October 2026 and December 2025 Angi reviews are entirely positive, noting the electrician was on time and stating they will call him for future work. The lead rests on the forgotten follow-up in August 2019 and the exact same follow-up and ducking-calls complaint surfacing again in June 2026.

## Why it matters

For an electrician, the appointment is the job, and the inspection follow-up is the part the customer remembers. A panel upgrade is not finished until the city inspection is done. Forgetting this requirement, as admitted to Andrew U. in 2019, leaves a panel upgrade unfinished, because the job is not done until the city inspection is done.

Furthermore, a customer who cannot reach the business after the job is finished does not come back. The June 2026 review highlights the exact same complaint about unreturned calls and dropped follow-ups seven years after the 2019 reviews, indicating a persistent gap in the company's communication process.

## The proposed solution

The proposed solution implements an automated communication sequence and an internal tracking board.

When a customer books a panel upgrade or an EV-charger install, the system sends an SMS confirming the appointment. A day-before reminder and a morning "we're on our way" text follow, ensuring the customer is never left waiting in silence. A missed-call log records every missed call so a customer who calls back is never left unanswered. Finally, an inspection follow-up tracker keeps every panel-upgrade job on the board with its city-inspection step until the inspection is confirmed done. This ensures the step Andrew U. described can never be forgotten again.

What it does not do: This solution requires no dispatch overhaul, no CRM replacement, no pricing change, and no electrical work change.

## Scope, price, and terms

Scope: Implementation of the SMS appointment confirmation, day-before reminder, morning call-ahead text, missed-call log, and inspection follow-up tracker.

Price: $900 to $1,500 fixed fee. An optional $300 per month retainer is available for ongoing support.

Terms: 35/40/25 milestone schedule. This includes a 35 percent kickoff payment, 40 percent upon accepted working demo, and 25 percent on final delivery. Intellectual property transfers to MightyBee Electric, LLC on final payment.
