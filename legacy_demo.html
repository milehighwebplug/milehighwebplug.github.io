Legacy Moving LLC: move-day confirmation and a promised-callback log

Concept demo. When a customer books a move, the system confirms the move day and window by SMS, sends a day-before reminder, and texts "crew en route" on the morning of the move. If the crew runs late, the move is rescheduled, or the window is missed, it texts the customer automatically instead of leaving them waiting in silence. Every booked move is on the day's move board, and every missed call and every promised callback is in the log, so a callback that was promised can never fall out of the process unnoticed again.
