MightyBee Electric: appointment confirmation and inspection follow-up

Concept demo. When a customer books a panel upgrade or an EV-charger install, the system confirms the appointment by SMS, sends a day-before reminder, and texts "we're on our way" on the morning of the visit. A missed-call log records every missed call so a customer who calls back is never left unanswered, and an inspection follow-up tracker keeps every panel-upgrade job on the board with its city-inspection step until the inspection is confirmed done. A panel upgrade is not finished until the city inspection is done, and that step was forgotten once already.
